# UH-INT-03 — Ceremonial Hall

## 1. Hotspot Identity

- **Hotspot ID:** UH-INT-03
- **Landmark:** Urduja House
- **Section:** Interior
- **Hotspot Title:** Ceremonial Hall
- **Prompt / Trigger Label:** Explore the Ceremonial Hall
- **Interaction Type:** Interactive informational room hotspot
- **Priority:** Required interior hotspot
- **System Context:** AKAR — Web-Based Interactive Historical Walkthrough for Learning the History and Cultural Heritage of Lingayen, Pangasinan

---

## 2. Purpose of the Hotspot

This hotspot introduces the Ceremonial Hall as an important functional interior space inside Urduja House. It should help visitors understand that Urduja House is not only historically significant but also remains connected to present-day provincial governance through selected official functions and receptions.

This hotspot must support the educational goals of AKAR by:

- explaining the role of the Ceremonial Hall;
- showing that the hall continues to be used for official events;
- allowing the visitor to inspect the hall visually in an engaging but simple way;
- maintaining historical and present-day relevance without inventing information.

---

## 3. Educational Focus

The hotspot should help the visitor recognize that:

- the Ceremonial Hall is a formal interior space used for selected receptions and official functions;
- the continuing use of the hall demonstrates that Urduja House remains active in provincial governance;
- the hall connects the residence’s historical role with present-day public service and leadership.

### Learning Takeaway
**Urduja House continues to participate in provincial governance through ceremonial and official functions.**

---

## 4. Approved Historical / Educational Content

### 4.1 Main Approved Description
Use the following approved content exactly unless a researcher later provides a revised validated version:

> The Ceremonial Hall is used on selected special occasions for official receptions and government functions. Its continuing use demonstrates that Urduja House remains an active part of provincial governance. The hall connects the historical role of the residence with present-day public service and provincial leadership.

### 4.2 Approved Narration Text
Use the following narration text exactly unless a researcher later supplies a revised validated script:

> The Ceremonial Hall continues to serve as a venue for selected official events and government functions. Its use shows that Urduja House is not simply a preserved historical building. It remains an active setting for provincial leadership and public service.

---

## 5. Interaction Concept

This hotspot must be **interactive, touch-friendly, landscape-first, and consistent with UH-INT-01 and UH-INT-02**.

It must not feel like a static information page.

The interaction uses **three selectable sections**:

1. **ABOUT THE HALL**
2. **OFFICIAL EVENTS**
3. **VIEW THE SPACE**

Each section should update the content area while preserving a consistent overall layout.

---

## 6. UX / Design Rules

### 6.1 Platform / Experience Rules
- Designed primarily for **landscape touchscreen tablets**
- Must also remain usable for **QR-accessed phone users in landscape**
- Do **not** redesign into portrait
- Keep touch-first controls
- Avoid hover-only interactions
- Keep controls readable and large
- Maintain the AKAR visual identity

### 6.2 Consistency Requirements
The UI must visually align with previous hotspots:
- dark historical panel background
- light/gold accent selection states
- same or similar button styling
- same speaker icon system used in earlier interior hotspots
- visible Close and Sources controls
- smooth but simple transitions
- no game-like mechanics

### 6.3 Accessibility / Interaction Rules
- minimum touch-friendly controls around **48–56 logical pixels**
- keyboard activation should still function where practical
- clear selected-state highlighting
- no requirement to complete all sections in order
- no achievements, scoring, or progress gating

---
